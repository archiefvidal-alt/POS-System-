Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Set the production cost for each product in Products. COGS is calculated from the quantities sold.

To enter or change a cost: open Products → Add Product / Edit, then fill in Cost per Item (₱). The Income Statement multiplies each sold quantity by its saved item cost.

COGS is calculated automatically from recorded sales and each product's cost per item.
